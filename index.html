General Provisions

Epohers World Cup is an esports entertainment event – an Age of Empires IV tournament hosted by the Epohers team.
All participants must abide by these regulations.
The tournament organiser is the Epohers team. Tournament admins: Tosic (Discord: @tosic_graf) and Shtoprrrr (Discord: @shtoprrrr).
All tournament communication takes place on the Epohers Discord server: https://discord.gg/WWt2VUncCJ.
The organisers reserve the right to amend the rules at any time; significant changes will be announced publicly.
Any issues not covered by these rules will be decided exclusively by the organisers.

TOURNAMENT FORMAT

Qualifiers

Two single-elimination brackets for 48 players.

12 players advance to the main stage.

All matches are Best-of-3

Group Stage

Sixteen players in four GSL-style groups.

Top two from each group advance.

All matches are Best-of-5

Playoffs

Single-elimination bracket for eight players.

Includes a 3rd-place match.

Best-of-5 & Best-of-7

REGISTRATION & SEEDING

Registration

Complete the registration form (link).
Join the Discord server – https://discord.gg/WWt2VUncCJ.
Ensure your name appears on the tournament registration list.
Registration closes on August 29 at 20:00 GMT.

Seeding

External seeding is used only for placing players in qualifier brackets. It is based on Tournament ELO (or 1v1 ranked ELO if none exists); earlier registrants may gain an advantage.
Internal seeding means the seed position is fixed.
Four invited players take the first seed in each group (ranked by EGC Masters Points).
Qualified players are placed into groups using a reverse seeding principle.
Playoff pairings are based on group results: 1A vs 2C, 1D vs 2B, 1B vs 2D, 1C vs 2A.

MAPS & CIVILIZATIONS RULES

Common info

Maps are drafted before civilizations.
The higher-seeded player chooses to be the host or guest for the draft.

Maps

Players must draft maps according to the official draft links.
The last map drawn is always played first.
After the first map, the loser of the previous game chooses the next map from the remaining pool.
Maps cannot be repeated within a series.
A player may select maps from their opponent’s list.
Map pool:
Dry Arabia
Cliffside
EGC – Gorge
EGC – Holy Island
EGC – Archipelago
Baldland
Hill and Dale

Civilizations

The handbook does not specify a civilization draft. Use standard Age of Empires IV civ drafting rules or await official presets.

SERVER & LOBBY CONFIGURATION

Server Rules

Each player must have a stable internet connection; no other party is responsible for connection quality.
Players must agree on a server before hosting.
If players cannot agree, either flip a coin to decide the server for the first game and alternate thereafter, or allow a tournament admin to select a geographically fair server.

GAME RULES

In-game rules

Players may not construct stone walls before reaching the Castle Age (Age III).

Game restarts

A game may be restarted at any time by mutual agreement of the players, but only once per map.
A game may be restarted once per map if a player experiences a technical issue within the first two minutes (a screenshot must be sent to the admin and opponent).
In all other cases, only tournament administrators may decide to restart a game.

Game mods

GAME EXPLOITS & MISCONDUCT

Cheating and exploits

Cheating or hacking is strictly prohibited; the use of any external software to gain an advantage is forbidden.
Using bugs, glitches, exploits or other unauthorized methods to gain an advantage is prohibited.
Known exploits include:
Using an unfinished building with zero villager build time to tether sheep.
Intentionally denying fish by placing building foundations on them.
Intentionally cancelling buildings with Jeanne d’Arc to gain extra experience.
Unpacking and repacking the Khaganate Palace to reset unit production.

Misconduct and disrespect

All players are expected to show respect to other participants, organisers, spectators and anyone involved in the event.
Impersonating another person or misleading the organisers and participants by changing nicknames or accounts is prohibited.
Receiving external assistance during matches (verbal hints, coaching, remote control, etc.) is not allowed.
