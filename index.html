TOURNAMENT FORMAT

Qualifiers

Single-elimination 48-6 bracket formed from registered players. Top 48 players by seeding are able to participate.
There are 2 qualifiers. From each qualifier 6 players will advance to the main stage (summary 12).
4 players are invited directly and are considered qualified.
All Qualifier matches are Best of 3.

Group Stage

16 qualified players are seeded into 4 groups of 4 each one.
Group Stage format is GSL:
First round: top-seeded vs fourth-seeded, second-seeded vs third-seeded.
Second round: winners match – winner advances to playoffs with 1st group-place, loser match – loser eliminated.
Third round: deciding match between remaining players – winner advances with 2nd group-place, loser eliminated.
All Group Stage matches are Best of 5.

Playoffs

Single-elimination 8 bracket with 3rd place match formed from players advanced from Group Stage.
First round seeding is 1st group-place players vs 2nd group-place players.
Quarterfinals and 3rd place match are Best of 5.
Semifinals and grand final are Best of 7.

Registration Procedure

Complete these steps to register for the tournament:

Verify your name appears in the registration list – Registration List Link
Registration deadline: August 29, 20:00 GMT

Seeding System

The tournament uses two types of seeding:
External seeding - Used only for qualifier bracket seeding:
Based on Tournament ELO (or ranked 1v1 ELO for players without Tournament ELO)
Registration order serves as an additional seeding factor
Internal seeding - Used within the tournament:
The 4 invited players receive 1st seed in groups (using EGC Masters Points)
Qualified players are seeded into groups by reverse seeding principle
Playoffs seeding is based on group stage results

MAPS & CIVILIZATIONS RULES

Map Rules

In the match draft, maps are drafted first, then civilizations are drafted.
Match drafts are hosted by the higher seed player.
Game drafts host alternates starting with the higher seed player.
Maps cannot be repeated in the same series.

Civilization Rules

Players draft civilizations after map selection is complete.
Each player bans one civilization at the beginning of each series, which cannot be picked throughout the entire match.
Civilizations cannot be repeated in Best of 3 and Best of 5 matches.

Required Mods

The following mods must be downloaded, installed, and verified before tournament participation:
EGC - Gorge
Baldland
EGC - Holy Island
EGC - Archipelago

GAME RULES

In-Game Rules

  • Players are not allowed to construct stone walls before the Castle Age (Age 3).
  • Players are not allowed to intentionally remove fish food sources by means of palisades (wooden walls).

Game Restarts

  • Game restart may be made at any time by mutual agreement of the players in the current match, but only one per map.
  • Game restart may be made if one of the players has a technical error within the first 2 minutes after the game starts (the error must be documented by screenshot and sent to tournament admin and opponent).
  • In all other cases, the decision to restart the map is made exclusively by tournament administrators.

See required mods on page 7.

GAME EXPLOITS & MISCONDUCT

Prohibited Actions

  • Third-party software that provides any advantage in the game is strictly prohibited.
  • Any use of bugs, exploits, or other unauthorized methods to gain advantages is strictly forbidden.

List of Known Exploits

  • Cancellation of attack animation for all ranged cavalry units
  • Cancellation of attack animation (including torch throws) by placing units in buildings or transports (battering rams, siege towers, ships, etc.)
  • Using unfinished buildings with zero builder time spent on construction for tethering sheep

Penalties and Reporting

  • Players found using any of these or other exploits will face penalties, including potential disqualification from the tournament.
  • All incidents of suspected cheating must be reported to tournament admins with appropriate evidence (screenshots, recordings).

FAIR PLAY & MISCONDUCT

Code of Conduct

  • Respectful conduct is required. Disrespect towards other participants, organizers, spectators, or anyone involved in the tournament is prohibited.
  • Smurfing is prohibited. Using alternative accounts to gain advantages during registration or seeding will result in disqualification.
  • Impersonation is prohibited. Misleading organizers and other participants by changing your nickname, account, or other identifying information is grounds for disqualification.

External Assistance

  • External assistance during tournament games is prohibited, including:
    • Verbal assistance (coaching, hints)
    • Physical assistance (account sharing, remote access)

Sanctions

Players who behave in a manner that threatens the progress, integrity, or outcome of the event may be sanctioned with prize money reduction, disqualification, or ban from future tournaments.
