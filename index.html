6.2 Seeding System

The tournament uses two types of seeding:
External seeding - Used only for qualifier bracket seeding:
Based on Tournament ELO (or ranked 1v1 ELO for players without Tournament ELO)
Registration order serves as an additional seeding factor
Internal seeding - Used within the tournament:
The 4 invited players receive 1st seed in groups (using EGC Masters Points)
Qualified players are seeded into groups by reverse seeding principle
Playoffs seeding is based on group stage results

MAPS & CIVILIZATIONS RULES

7.1 Map Rules

In the match draft, maps are drafted first, then civilizations are drafted.
Match drafts are hosted by the higher seed player.
Game drafts host alternates starting with the higher seed player.
Maps cannot be repeated in the same series.

7.2 Civilization Rules

Players draft civilizations after map selection is complete.
Each player bans one civilization at the beginning of each series, which cannot be picked throughout the entire match.
Civilizations cannot be repeated in Best of 3 and Best of 5 matches.

7.3 Required Mods

The following mods must be downloaded, installed, and verified before tournament participation:
EGC - Gorge
Baldland
EGC - Holy Island
EGC - Archipelago

GAME RULES

9.1 In-Game Rules

🚫

Players are not allowed to construct stone walls before the Castle Age (Age 3).

🚫

Players are not allowed to intentionally remove fish food sources by means of palisades (wooden walls).

9.2 Game Restarts

🔄

Game restart may be made at any time by mutual agreement of the players in the current match, but only one per map.

🔄

Game restart may be made if one of the players has a technical error within the first 2 minutes after the game starts (the error must be documented by screenshot and sent to tournament admin and opponent).

GAME EXPLOITS & MISCONDUCT

10.1 Prohibited Actions

🚫

Third-party software that provides any advantage in the game is strictly prohibited.

🚫

Any use of bugs, exploits, or other unauthorized methods to gain advantages is strictly forbidden.

10.2 List of Known Exploits

🐛

Cancellation of attack animation for all ranged cavalry units

🐛

Cancellation of attack animation (including torch throws) by placing units in buildings or transports (battering rams, siege towers, ships, etc.)

10.3 Penalties and Reporting

🚨

Players found using any of these or other exploits will face penalties, including potential disqualification from the tournament. All incidents of suspected cheating must be reported to tournament admins with appropriate evidence (screenshots, recordings).
