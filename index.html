3 TOURNAMENT FORMAT

3.1 General information

Tournament in single format - 1v1.
The tournament consists of 3 stages: qualifiers, group stage and playoffs.

3.2 Qualifiers

Single-elimination 40-5 bracket formed from registered players.

Top 40 players by seeding are able to participate.

There are two qualifiers, 5 players advance from each (10 total).

Six players are invited directly.

First and second round matches are Best-of-3
Third (Final) round matches are Best-of-5

3 TOURNAMENT FORMAT

3.3 Group stage

Group stage format is GSL.

Sixteen players are seeded into four groups of four.

Top two from each group advance.

First round matches: top-seeded vs fourth-seeded, second-seeded vs third-seeded.
Second round matches: winners match – the winner advanced to playoffs with 1st group-place, loser match – the loser eliminated from the tournament.
Third round: deciding match between two remaining players – winner advanced to playoffs with 2nd group-place, loser left the tournament.

All matches are Best-of-5

← means advancing to the next stage
← means elimination

3.4 Playoffs

Single-elimination bracket for eight players with a 3rd-place match.

Quarters/3rd Place: Best-of-5

Semis/Grand Final: Best-of-7

4.2 Seeding system

The tournament uses 2 types of seeding: external and internal. External is used for starting seed in qualifiers, internal is used only within the tournament.

External seeding

External seeding is used only for qualifier bracket seed.
External seeding is based on the standard reverse seeding principle.
Parameters of external seeding, by priority:
tournament ELO;
ranked ELO 1x1;
order of registration.

Internal seeding

Internal seed depends only on the player's success in the tournament and does not depend on the external seed with which the player came.

Invited players: the following persons are directly invited to the tournament:
Winner of the previous Epohers World Cup.
Top four players of the last S-tier tournament.
Top players by Tournament ELO.
Group placement: the 6 invited players seed in 4 groups (Groups A-D), ranked according to their Tournament ELO.
Qualified players: players who qualify are seeded into groups depending on which slot they receive in the qualifiers, using the reverse seeding principle.
Playoffs seeding based on group stage results:
1A vs 2C
1B vs 2D
1D vs 2B
1C vs 2A

7 GAME RULES

7.1 In-game rules

Players may not construct stone walls before reaching the Castle Age (Age III).

7.2 Game restarts

A game may be restarted at any time by mutual agreement of the players, but only once per map.
A game may be restarted once per map if a player experiences a technical issue within the first two minutes after start of the game (a screenshot must be sent to the tournament admin and opponent).
In all other cases, the decision to restart the game is made exclusively by tournament administrators.

8 SPORTSMANSHIP

8.1 Cheating and exploits

Cheating or hacking is not allowed. The use of any external software to achieve victory or gain an advantage in the game is prohibited.
The use of any bugs, glitches, exploits and other unauthorized methods to achieve victory or gain an advantage in the game is prohibited.
List of known exploits:
Cancel attack animation (including torch throwing) by placing units into buildings or transports (rams, siege towers, ships, etc.).
Using an unfinished building with zero villager build time to tether sheep.
Intentionally denying fish by placing building foundations on top of them.
Intentionally cancelling buildings with Jeanne d’Arc in order to gain more experience.
Unpack and repack the Khaganate Palace in order to reset unit production.

8.2 Bad manners and fair play

All players must be respectful to tournament participants, organizers, casters, spectators and any other people involved in the event.
Smurfing, using another person's account to gain an advantage during registration or seeding is forbidden.
Impersonating another person or misleading the organizers and other tournament participants by changing nicknames, accounts, etc., is prohibited.
Using external assistance during tournament matches, such as verbal (hints, coaching) or physical (transferring control, including remote access), is prohibited.
